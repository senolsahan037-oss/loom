# Two corpora, kept apart on purpose

`corpus/` — the product's musical intelligence. Evidence about how hit records of
the last thirty years are actually built, per genre. This is what ships and what
Loom reasons from for any user.

`personal/` — one user's own record collection, measured for their own sampling
work. It is that person's taste and their source material.

They must never be merged. A genre model trained on one listener's shelf learns
that listener, and then presents it to everyone else as evidence.
